# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone.

Participation in this community should be harassment-free regardless of:

- Experience
- Gender
- Identity
- Nationality
- Religion
- Disability
- Age

---

## Expected Behaviour

Be respectful.

Be constructive.

Be inclusive.

Assume good intentions.

Provide helpful feedback.

Welcome newcomers.

Respect differing opinions.

---

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Harassment

Discrimination

Personal attacks

Trolling

Threats

Publishing private information

Spam

Any behaviour that negatively impacts the community.

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject content that violates this Code of Conduct.

Repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent bans.
